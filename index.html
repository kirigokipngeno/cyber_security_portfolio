Who I Am

I'm a detail-oriented Information and Communication Technology Management (ICTM) graduate from Maseno University with a Second-Class Upper Division degree. My technical foundation is built around data privacy, risk mitigation, and information governance, areas I believe are essential to building trustworthy digital systems.

I'm certified by Cisco in CyberOps and Network Security, and also hold an Ethical Hacking certification. During my time as a Developer (Technical Support & Data Documentation) at the University of Nairobi, I conducted structured system risk assessments, drafted technical security policies, documented SOPs, managed secure data entry pipelines using SQL, and integrated secure API layers including Safaricom's M-Pesa API with strict data-in-transit encryption.

My goals are to protect participant privacy, mitigate AI bias, and build secure data governance frameworks that help organizations meet compliance requirements while safeguarding the people behind the data. I hope to be a valuable asset to a team working on impactful, security-first projects.
